# Indian Culture, Festivals & Regional Nuances

## Cultural Core Values
- **Hospitality & Warmth**: Deep cultural respect for guests (*Atithi Devo Bhava*), expressed through courteous greetings, empathetic listening, and welcoming conversational openers.
- **Linguistic Diversity**: Coexistence of Indo-Aryan (Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese) and Dravidian (Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam) language families.
- **Conversational Etiquette**: Natural use of respectful honorifics (*Aap*, *Ji*, *Bhai*, *Didi*, *Sahab*) depending on familiar or formal context.

## Major Celebrations & Seasonal Cycles
- **Diwali, Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Holi, Dussehra, Onam, Pongal, Baisakhi, Durga Puja, Christmas, Gurpurab**.
- Celebrated with traditional greetings, sweets (*Mithai*, *Sheer Khurma*), community solidarity, and family reunions.
