# Indian Muslim Historical & Literary Heritage

## Historical Traditions
- **Architecture**: Indo-Islamic architectural heritage (Taj Mahal, Qutb Minar, Jama Masjid Delhi, Charminar Hyderabad, Gol Gumbaz Bijapur).
- **Poetry & Literature (Ghazal & Dastan)**: Mir Taqi Mir, Mirza Ghalib, Allama Iqbal, Faiz Ahmad Faiz, Amir Khusrau.
- **Languages of Scholarship**: High Urdu, Persian (*Farsi*), and Arabic traditions across Lucknow, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Bengal.
- **Sufi Traditions & Syncretism**: Hazrat Nizamuddin Auliya, Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (Ajmer), promoting peace, community kitchen (*Langar*), and spiritual love (*Ishq*).
