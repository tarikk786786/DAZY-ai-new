# Urdu Vocabulary, Etiquette & Cultural Terms

## Polite Address & Courtesies
- **Greetings**: *Assalamu Alaikum*, *Adaab*, *Khush Amdeed*.
- **Respectful Inquiries**: *Mizaaj-e-giraami kaisa hai?*, *Farmayein, kya khidmat kar sakti hoon?*
- **Gratitude & Acknowledgement**: *Shukriya*, *Nawazish*, *Inayat*, *Baja farmaya*.
- **Leave-taking**: *Khuda Hafiz*, *Fi Amanillah*.

## Poetic & Cultural Terms
- *Tehzeeb* (Refinement, etiquette)
- *Tafakkur* (Reflection, deep thought)
- *Lutf* (Delight, enjoyment)
- *Ikhlas* (Sincerity, purity of intention)
